Frequently asked questions

Clear information before you shop.

Is TROP Market the same company as Jumia?

No. TROP Market Uganda is an independent product-discovery website created by Agona Peter. Jumia is a separate retailer. Product links take you to Jumia's own pages.

Can I pay or place an order on TROP Market?

No. This version does not take payments or submit orders. To purchase, follow the product link and complete checkout on the retailer's website.

Why might the price on TROP be different?

Prices shown here are catalogue snapshots and do not update automatically from Jumia. Sale prices, availability, delivery fees and product options may change; the live retailer listing is the source to trust.

Are TROP reviews the same as Jumia verified ratings?

No. Reviews submitted on TROP are separate from Jumia's own ratings. TROP reviews may appear only after its review system has been connected and the website owner approves them.

Does TROP deliver products or offer returns?

TROP does not store or deliver the linked items. Check Jumia's delivery coverage, charges and return eligibility for the product and location you choose.
